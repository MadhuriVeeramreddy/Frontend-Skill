FAQ

Most asked questions.

It's the name, so we may as well answer them here. Anything missing — just ask.

01 What does a project cost?

Most sites land between £6k and £20k; MVPs and automation work are scoped per project. You get a fixed number before we start, not a rate card and a surprise.

02 How long until something is live?

A plan within a week of your first message. A working site in three to six weeks. MVPs usually take six to ten, depending on how much of it is genuinely new.

03 Who will I actually be talking to?

The people building it. A.M.A is small on purpose — there's no account manager between you and the work.

04 Can you work with our existing team?

Often. We'll take the part nobody has time for, work in your stack and your repo, and hand it back documented.

05 What happens after launch?

Thirty days of fixes are included. After that, keep us on a small monthly retainer or take the keys and run it yourself — both are fine.
